# Art direction / flavour brief

## One sentence

**A cheerful, handmade-looking civic project to murder God, besieged by horrifying-but-cute celestial geometry.**

## Tone

- Funny, defiant, cartoonish.
- Not grimdark.
- Not reverent faux-history and not a theology lesson.
- Humanity should look busy, practical and slightly overconfident.
- Heaven should look cosmically strange, officious and increasingly alarmed.
- The joke is mostly in juxtaposition: mundane public works versus impossible divine opposition.

## Core enemy silhouettes

### Ophanim Host
Standard enemy. Readable at tiny board-token scale.

Visual ingredients:
- concentric wheel/halo rings;
- many eyes;
- small unnecessary wings;
- bright gold/ivory divine ornament;
- slightly indignant personality.

Mechanics: Movement 1, one hit.

### Seraph
Late-game elite.

Visual ingredients:
- six wings wrapping a radiant central form;
- eyes peeking through feathers;
- taller/more vertical silhouette;
- brighter, hotter core;
- visibly shielded state that can be broken.

Mechanics: Movement 2, Shield 1, two hits total.

Do not add more enemy types until human play says the board needs them.

## Babel
Babel is the visual hero and should physically/digitally grow piece by piece.

Light stage labels:

1. Foundation of Defiance
2. The Great Ascent
3. The Siege of Heaven

Each stage should become more audacious rather than merely taller: rough civic masonry → monumental engineering → absurd celestial breach architecture.

When Heaven knocks off the newest piece, make it visibly painful and funny. This is a major emotional beat.

## Terrain readability

At gameplay scale, terrain identity beats illustration detail.

- Farmland: warm cultivated shapes.
- Forest: chunky clustered canopy.
- Hills: brick/clay earth forms.
- Mountain: hard mineral silhouette.
- Desert: pale barren sacrificial space.
- Rivers: strong continuous graphic line through tile centres/edges.

Buildings must sit clearly **on top of** terrain rather than visually becoming terrain themselves.

## Buildings

- harvesting buildings: charming practical industry;
- Towers: obvious defensive silhouette and player ownership;
- Walls: temporary-looking barricades/palisades, not giant castle walls.

## UI

Avoid faux-3D stone menus. Let the board be the tactile object; keep the surrounding UI flatter, cleaner and more graphic.

Resource cards should feel satisfying even digitally. Keep them readable and chunky rather than realistic.

## Confusion / Scheme flavour

This is where writing can be funniest. Keep names punchy and effects legible. One joke is enough; don't bury rules under flavour text.
